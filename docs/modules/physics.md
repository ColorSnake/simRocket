# Mechanics Overview

The simulation uses a 6 Degrees of Freedom (6DoF) rigid body dynamics model. The state of the rocket is defined by its position, velocity, orientation (quaternion), and angular velocity. The primary coordinate system is a flat-earth inertial frame where +Z is up, +X is North, and +Y is West.

## Equations of Motion

The state derivatives are computed as follows:

### Translational Dynamics
The translational acceleration is calculated using Newton's second law:
$$ a = \frac{F_{total}}{m} $$
Where $F_{total}$ is the sum of all forces in the inertial frame, and $m$ is the total mass of the rocket.

Forces considered:
1.  **Gravity**: $F_{gravity} = m \cdot g$ (where $g = [0, 0, -9.81]^T$)
2.  **Thrust**: Computed from the engine model and rotated by the TVC (Thrust Vector Control) actuators.
3.  **Aerodynamics**: Drag and normal forces.

### Rotational Dynamics
The rotational acceleration (angular acceleration $\alpha$) in the body frame is calculated using Euler's equations for rigid body dynamics:
$$ \alpha = I^{-1} \cdot (\tau_{total} - \omega \times (I \cdot \omega)) $$
Where:
*   $I$ is the inertia tensor matrix of the rocket.
*   $\tau_{total}$ is the total torque applied to the rocket.
*   $\omega$ is the angular velocity vector.

Torques considered:
1.  **TVC Torque**: Generated by the thrust vector not passing through the Center of Gravity (CG).
2.  **Aerodynamic Torque**: Generated by aerodynamic normal forces acting on the Center of Pressure (CP).
3.  **Damping Torque**: Artificial damping to simulate aerodynamic stability (pitch/yaw/roll).

### Kinematics
*   **Position**: $\frac{dp}{dt} = v$
*   **Velocity**: $\frac{dv}{dt} = a$
*   **Orientation (Quaternion)**: $\frac{dq}{dt} = \frac{1}{2} q \otimes \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \omega \end{bmatrix}$

## Integration
The simulation uses a 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) integrator to advance the state in time. The integration step is fixed at $dt = 0.001$ seconds (1000 Hz) to ensure numerical stability.

## Dynamic Mass Model
For liquid rockets, the mass and inertia change over time as propellant is consumed. The simulation calculates the instantaneous mass, CG, and inertia tensor by treating the rocket as a collection of point masses (dry mass) and cylindrical volumes (propellant tanks).

## State Summary (Workflow & Sampling Frequencies)

The main loop (`main.cpp`) is a deterministic **Lock-Step** loop. It guarantees that simulation steps are executed in a strict order, and simulation time is perceived equally by all components.

As seen above, different system components operate at virtually different sampling rates despite the physics colliding with continuous time at `1 ms` resolution:
- **Continuous Models (Physics, IMU, Aero, PID)**: Evaluated at every loop iteration (1000 Hz).
- **Delayed Sensors (GPS)**: The `update` function is called at 1000 Hz, but its internal logic only flushes a new message to the `MessageBus` once every `100 ms` (10 Hz).
- **Data Logging (UDP Telemetry/CSV)**: Down-sampled based on the configured `update_rate_hz` (e.g. 100 Hz) to prevent network congestion (UDP) and excessive log files, while maintaining high resolution for plots.
## Module Components

The physics module contains the core components simulating the natural phenomena and physical properties of the rocket.

## Integrators
- **`EulerIntegrator`**: Fast, simple, 1st-order solver. Used for rapid, low-fidelity simulations.
- **`RK4Integrator`**: Accurate, 4th-order Runge-Kutta solver. Recommended for precise flight dynamics.
  > [!WARNING]
  > **RK4 Stability Limits:** At extremely high dynamic pressures and very high aerodynamic damping coefficients (e.g., `pitch_yaw_damping_coefficient > 0.1`), RK4 can experience numerical instability (diverging to NaN values). Ensure damping coefficients are tuned properly (e.g. `0.02`) when flying through dense atmosphere at high speeds.

## Mass & Aerodynamics
- **`DynamicMassModel`**: Advanced model for liquid rockets (LRE). Treats tanks as cylinders (with `radius_m` and `height_m`). As propellant mass decreases, it calculates the shrinking height of the liquid cylinder and dynamically updates the full inertia tensor (`Ixx`, `Iyy`, `Izz`) and Center of Gravity (`CG`) using the Parallel Axis Theorem (Steiner's theorem).
- **`RigidBodyMassModel`**: (Legacy/Alternative) Simpler model for mass tracking.
- **`SimpleAerodynamicsModel`**: Computes aerodynamic drag and normal forces based on the Angle of Attack (AoA), dampening rotational rates, and applies torque at the Center of Pressure (COP).

## Environment
- **`SimpleEnvironmentModel`**: Simulates gravity vectors and 3D wind velocity profiles (crosswinds).

## Engines and Actuators
- **`IEngineModel`**: Base class for thrust generation. Computes scalar thrust and mass flow.
- **`SolidMotorModel`**: Simulates a solid rocket motor using `ThrustCurve` for realistic thrust profiles (supports `.eng` RASP format and `.csv`). Calculates dynamic mass depletion based on the integral of the thrust curve (Total Impulse).
- **`LiquidEngineModel`**: Simulates liquid rocket engines with throttle support (0-100% via MessageBus). It calculates variable thrust and mass flow, incorporating ambient pressure adjustments (Sea-Level vs Vacuum thrust limits).
- **`ThrustCurve`**: Utility class that parses motor files, interpolates thrust over time, and computes total impulse.
- **`IActuatorModel`**: Base class for mechanical linkages. Maps the generated thrust vector through 3D space (`TvcActuatorModel` uses quaternions to pitch/yaw the thrust around a pivot point).
